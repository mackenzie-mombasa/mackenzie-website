MACKENZIE APARTMENTS WEBSITE

index.html and the folders apartments, amenities, marina, contact are the public pages.
They are generated: do not edit them by hand.

draft.html is the working copy, with the review tools (phone/computer switch, photo picker).
Changes are made in draft.html, then tools\build.ps1 regenerates the public pages.

To look at the site offline: double-click index.html (public version) or draft.html (working copy).
img holds the photos, fonts holds the fonts. Keep the folders together.
